How an engagement runs

Four phases, in this order, every time. We don't start building until phase one is signed off by you.

Phase 1

Assess

Interviews with your team, a review of existing data and tools, and a scored map of where AI helps and where it doesn't.

Phase 2

Design

A specific plan for the highest-scoring opportunity: what gets built, what it replaces or touches, and what success looks like in numbers.

Phase 3

Build

We build in the open, with a working version in your hands inside the first two weeks so you're never reviewing something finished for the first time.

Phase 4

Operate

Handover, training, and a 30-day check-in to confirm the system is actually being used the way it was designed to be.

3–4

Client engagements run at a time, by design, so senior consultants stay hands-on through the whole build.

2 wks

Typical time from signed assessment to the first working version a client can click through.

100%
